Master's Thesis — Road Anomaly Detection using Smartphone Sensors
Dept. of Electrical Engineering, IIT Madras · Advisors: Dr. Boby George, Dr. Chinthaka Gooneratne
Aug 2023 – May 2024
  • Developed a physics-aware neural network for detecting road-surface anomalies from smartphone sensor data collected while riding a vehicle.
  • Analyzed filtering and maxima-tracing methods for peak-preserving de-noising of sensor data.
  • Modelled vehicle vibrations as a spring-mass system and fused physics-based predictions into an LSTM, achieving accurate predictions with low data requirements.
