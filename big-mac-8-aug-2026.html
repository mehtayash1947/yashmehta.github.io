The Big MAC and its different flavours

Yash Mehta · 12 August 2026 · 20 min read
Compute-in-Memory Analog SRAM Design

We all love the feeling of the biting into the soft bun followed by the crunchy patty and flavourful sauces of a McDonald's Big MAC burger. But the MAC operation is also a favourite among people who design ML accelerators and Compute-In-Memory architectures. That's because, not only is it the most widely used operation in most Machine Learning algorithms, but it is also one of the most expensive operations (not unlike the Big MAC, which may be priced reasonably but can be very expensive for your health).
